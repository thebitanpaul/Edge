## What's changed in Edge 1.0.2

### Smoother pointer over Wi-Fi
- The pointer on the other Mac no longer hesitates and then jumps when you start moving after a pause, or right after crossing over. Edge now keeps both Macs' Wi-Fi awake while the pointer is on the other one, and sends its input in Wi-Fi's higher-priority queue.

### Fixes
- No more stale pointer left standing at the edge of the screen you just left.

Update **both Macs** to 1.0.2 to get both fixes. For the smoothest pointer of all, connect the two Macs with a Thunderbolt or USB-C cable and choose **Connect over ▸ Cable** in the Edge menu.

**Full changelog:** https://github.com/thebitanpaul/Edge/compare/v1.0.1...v1.0.2
